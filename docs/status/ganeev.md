## Decisions
- **G11: three genuine false flags found by the nuisance tests, and fixed.** (a) **Glare:** a blown-out band across the forehead (57% of the skin sample) carries no echo, so the face answered 0.13x the neck and `light.face_neck` flagged `light.ratio`. Region means now leave out pixels with any channel >= 250 while at least 20% of the region stays clean (`vision/frames.py`, `CLIP_CODE`, `MIN_UNCLIPPED`); a region blown out beyond that keeps every pixel, so the quality gate still sees it clipped. (b) **JPEG quality 50:** the encoder wiped the sensor noise (neck and background noise 0), so `sensor.regions` flagged; it now abstains `quality.heavy_compression` under quality 80 (`min_jpeg_quality`), estimated from the frame's luminance quantisation table against the IJG table (`vision/frames.jpeg_quality`, within 2 of the true quality from 30 to 95). (c) **The same:** every pair of frames differed by under 0.1 code, so `sensor.duplicates` counted 81% repeats; a repeat must now be an exact copy or under `rel_mad` (0.1) x the session's median frame difference. A relay repeating every 10th frame exactly still flags; the synthetic virtual camera's 96% exact copies still flag.
- **G11: the 8 golden cases (Keshav's #42) keep every decision and every existing result** with #35, #38, G8, G7 and G11 together; the only change is 4 new result rows per session (`sensor.mode_probe`, `sensor.regions`, `sensor.prnu`, `visual.sbi`). So `golden.json` needs regenerating once G8/G7 and #42 are both on main.
- **G11 speed:** per 901 frames at 640x480, warm: PRNU 3.4 s (was 10.1 s: float32 and the noise median over every 2nd row and column), region means 3.7 s, region texture about 2 s, decoding 0.8 s. Not measured: the MediaPipe landmark pass on real faces (synthetic faces are not detected; with no face it costs nothing), estimated 5-15 s for 900 frames; SBI adds about 2 s. Total stays well under 90 s, so the landmark pass is not parallelised.
- **G7: preprocessing matches the SBI repo, not the task's "1.3x, square".** Read from `src/inference/preprocess.py` (`crop_face(..., crop_by_bbox=True, phase="test")`) and `inference_image.py`: the face box grows by 1/8 of its width left and right and 1/8 of its height top and bottom (1.25x), clipped to the frame, **not squared**, then `cv2.resize` to 380x380; RGB, CHW, float 0..1 with no normalisation; P(fake) = `softmax[:, 1]`. Our box is MediaPipe's landmark box, not RetinaFace's: close, not identical. SBI's own video score is the mean over frames (`sbi_mean`, reported); risk is the task's mean of the top 3 of 8 crops.
- **G7: no download, no pickled code.** The network is `EfficientNet.from_name("efficientnet-b4", num_classes=2)` (the architecture of SBI's `from_pretrained(..., advprop=True)`, whose ImageNet weights the checkpoint overwrites anyway), and the checkpoint is read with `torch.load(weights_only=True)`; the `net.` prefix of SBI's `Detector` is stripped. A broken checkpoint gives no model (logged), never a crash.
- **G7: crops prefer still, near-frontal frames** (outside every step, within 20 degrees of the median yaw), as SBI was trained on mostly frontal FF++ video and the colour steps tint the face; it falls back to any face frame when fewer than 8 qualify.
- **G7 speed (random weights, same network):** load 0.6 s, 8 crops 1.2-1.4 s on this laptop's CPU (14 threads). Measure again with the real weights.
- **G7: torch on Linux.** PyPI's Linux torch wheel pulls CUDA (about 2 GB); `analysis.txt` says to use the CPU index there. Windows and macOS wheels are CPU-only.
- **G8: `sensor.mode_probe` reads Yash's `ModeProbe` rows as they are.** A refused mode and one the browser could not ask for (no resizeMode: Safari, Firefox) both arrive as `accepted: false`, so an empty probe **or one with nothing accepted** abstains (`sensor.probe_not_run`: unsupported, not failed). Too perfect (risk 0.7) uses only the unusual 1000x750 row: accepted, delivered at that size, `switch_ms` < 150 and `frames_missing` == 0. Phantom switch (risk 1): accepted, no frame of the new size within the probe's 1 s, and still delivering the preflight's working size. **Both are hypotheses for virtual cameras; measure at S2**: a real camera slower than 1 s to reconfigure would look like a phantom switch, and `frames_missing` is Yash's estimate (expected frames at the old fps minus frames seen), so check it is 0 on Chromium's fake camera before trusting "too perfect".
- **G8: `sensor.regions` scores only the noise ratios** (face/neck and face/background, the worse one; risk from |ln ratio| beyond ln 2.5, ln 2 per unit, flagged from 3.5x either way). The sharpness ratio is reported, not scored: skin and a patterned shirt differ in texture for real. Still frames only (baseline and gaps: from 100 ms before a step to 400 ms after it, the turn to ended + 500 ms). Abstains on `quality.no_face`, `moving`, `neck_hidden` or `clipped` in the "all" window, or under 20 still frames with a face.
- **G8: noise is a trimmed RMS, not a MAD.** Frame differences are integer codes; with the synthetic default noise (0.003, under one 8-bit code) a MAD snapped to the code steps, so a face at 0.6x the neck's noise read 0.9x and at 0.25x read 0. The spread of the central 95% around the median (/0.871) reads 0.25x as 0.20x and 4x as 3.8x at noise 0.02 (about 4-5 codes, like a webcam). Tests use noise 0.02; at 0.003 the 8-bit rounding and JPEG dominate (0.25x reads 0.69x), which no real camera does.
- **G8: `sensor.prnu` sets `voting=False` in code**, not only in `thresholds.yaml`, so it can never flag. The synthetic sessions all give a high PCE (their pixel noise comes from a fixed bank of 16 fields, a pattern by construction; the noise-free virtual camera repeats an identical scene), so the check is tested on injected fingerprints and the vision code on the old repo's pattern / no pattern / static scene frames.
- **G8: the old noise curve (`NoiseAccumulator`, `fit_channel`) is not ported.** No check in CONTRACT section 9 uses it; `vision/noise.py` holds the per-region texture instead.
- **G8 cost:** texture and fingerprint add about 2-3 ms per frame at 320x240 (404-frame synthetic `analyze()` about 3 s). The 640x480, 900-frame budget is measured in G11.
- **#33 fix: gaps the camera is not to blame for are left out of `sensor.cadence`.** (a) Frames lost in upload: the browser numbers every frame it grabs, so an `idx` jump across a gap means frames were captured but never arrived (`integrity.coverage` reports them); the interval is left out of the timing statistics, the count goes to `frames_missing`, and the result notes `quality.frames_missing`. (b) A hidden page: skipped events carry no time, but the client checks `document.hidden` as each step is due, so a gap spanning the scheduled start (± 250 ms) of a step skipped with `page_hidden` is left out too (`quality.page_hidden`). Only a gap with consecutive `idx` away from a hidden page is `sensor.stall`. Duplicates are unaffected (no frame is compared across a missing one as a repeat). Also removed the #5 workaround (`context.read_frame`): Keshav's fix is merged, `Bundle.read_frame` returns None for an empty JPEG.
- **#17 fix: a weak genuine echo is unclear, never silent.** Three changes, all towards abstaining: (a) `echo.echo_onset` marks a step invalid when its baseline drifts (straight-line trend over the 500 ms before `painted` moves more than max(4 sigma around the trend, `a_min`) over 300 ms; 500 ms because 300 ms holds 4-5 frames at 15 fps, which rejected 12-17% of steady steps; at 500 ms, 0 of 576); (b) `light.response` pools the steps without an echo (Stouffer z of each step's mean push towards its colour, `echo.hold_z`): `faint_echo_z` >= 2.5 means the face answered below the onset threshold, so those steps are "faint" (unclear), not silent; (c) an answered step whose colour change is under `order_min_snr` (2) x its threshold is "weak_colour" (unclear); only a clear change counts as wrong order. `light.face_neck`: a face with `hold_z` >= `faint_z` (3) is not silent, so the step is not neck-only. **Synthetic, before vs after** (6 gains x 3 noises x 2 fps x 3 seeds): genuine with `echo_gain` 0.03-0.06 flagged 15 of 54 on main, 0 after (they abstain with `light.weak_face_echo`); replay and swap flag 9 of 18 each, unchanged (their pooled z stays within -2.1..1.9). face_neck: genuine faint face + shirt 8x flagged 1 of 6 on main, 0 after; swaps unchanged. **Trade-off:** a replay whose screen reflects our colours (glare) can now look faint and abstain; `light.background` is the check for a glossy screen. **Check on real sessions at S2:** how often real cameras drift before a step (each drifting step is left out), and `faint_echo_z` on genuine vs replay.
- **G6 fix (before merging #11): the turn is judged before the face loss, and a dark or clipped turn window is unable to judge.** (a) Wrong direction and followed are now decided first, from the frames where the face was seen; `turn.face_lost` (risk 0.6) applies only when those frames show no turn either way. Synthetic: 60% of the turn lost around the middle still shows a 29-degree turn, so it is followed (risk 0), and a wrong-way turn with the same loss stays `turn.wrong_direction`; at 80% lost nothing is seen and it is `turn.face_lost`. (b) A turn window with `quality.too_dark` or `quality.clipped` abstains with that reason ("Unable to judge the turn: …"), even before a wrong-way turn: the yaw of an unlit or blown-out face is not evidence (AGENTS.md rule 3). `quality.too_bright` without clipping still runs. 4 new tests; full suite 166 passed, 1 skipped (face model not fetched), lint clean.
- **G6: two outcomes the task does not cover.** (a) Turned the right way (≥ 15 degrees) but still more than 10 degrees away at the end: usable, risk 0, new code `turn.no_return` (noted, not scored: a person may be slow to turn back). (b) A partial turn (10–15 degrees, or both ways under 15): abstain `turn.not_performed`, like no movement.
- **G6: `turn.face_lost` flags as the task says (risk 0.6 ≥ flag_at 0.5), but it pulls against AGENTS.md rule 3** (poor capture never flags): a person who leans out of frame during the turn also loses the face. Its effect is limited (one family flagging → `RETRY_STEP_UP` with a fresh challenge, not human review). **Decide at S2/S3** whether it votes, once genuine sessions show how often a face is lost for more than 40% of the turn.
- **G6: the latency is interpolated to the 10-degree crossing** between face frames (as `echo_onset` does), not the first frame above it: frames are 30–100 ms apart. A fast start (10 degrees at ~133 ms) gives `turn.too_fast`, which is measured, not scored.
- **G6: abstains** with `quality.low_fps` when the turn window is below 12 fps, with `quality.no_face` when there is no face in the 500 ms before the prompt or less than 80% of the turn without movement, and with `turn.not_performed` when the prompt was not shown (a skipped event).
- **G6: turn.follow does not catch a replay of a session that turned** (the synthetic replay has the same yaw): it only checks that a head follows *this* prompt. Replays are family C's job.
- **G5: the neck-only rule keeps the old repo's majority condition** (neck-only steps ≥ 2 **and** at least half of the measured steps), on top of the task's "≥ 2", so one or two odd steps cannot flag a genuine person.
- **G5: the face–neck delay is reported (`median_cf_delta_ms`), not scored,** as the task says. V2 scored it; one webcam frame is longer than any lag limit.
- **G5: the ratio rule rarely fires.** A pair needs both echoes at ≥ 2× their threshold, so a neck far weaker than the face usually becomes "too close to the noise" (abstain, `light.weak_echoes`) before its ratio can pass ln 4 + ½ ln 2 (5.66×). Synthetic: neck 0.1× at noise 0.003 abstains; neck 0.08× at noise 0.001 flags at 6.2×. Strong necks saturate in chromaticity (10× the screen light gives only a 0.39 ratio), so the high side does not flag either.
- **G5: a swap can lose a step to "unclear":** a face noise frame peaking over its threshold makes that step not clearly silent (ported rule). The synthetic swap flags on 5 of 6.
- **G5: light.background projects both regions' mean change onto the shown colour** (`echo.response`) over steps with a clear face echo; risk = 0.5 × ratio / 0.8. Genuine synthetic ratio is 0.07, a full-frame glossy screen (wall echo = face echo) 1.07. A real replay may differ; measure at S2. New code `light.background_hidden` (under 20% of the frame is background).
- **G5: new code `light.weak_echoes`** for face_neck, because the weak side may be the neck, not the face.
- **Test speed: 107 s → 50 s.** `server/tests/analysis/conftest.py` gives a session-scoped `synth` fixture: `synth.session(**kw)` renders each distinct set of `make_session` arguments once (never modify its files), and `synth.context(**kw)` hands out a deep copy of the built context. Tests that corrupt or copy frames keep their own `make_session(tmp_path, ...)`. Also: region means of a uint8 frame now go through a 256-bin histogram per channel and the linear lookup table (identical values, tested to 1e-12), so `build_context` no longer builds a float image (1.05 s → 0.65 s for 284 frames; this also counts towards G11's 90 s budget). And the fixture's pixel noise comes from random crops of a fixed noise bank (render 1.29 s → 0.77 s). The synthetic matrix is unchanged.
- **G2: `WindowQuality.usable` ignores the neck; `blocking(neck=True)` adds it.** The neck rule only matters for checks that use the neck (face_neck, sensor.regions), as the task says. `quality.neck_hidden` is always in `reasons`.
- **G2: the motion rule does not apply in a turn window, and the turn's frames are left out of "all"'s motion,** so asking the person to turn never makes the whole session "moving".
- **G2: luma is the sRGB luma (0..255) of the skin region's mean linear colour; `luma_ok` is over face frames only** (no face is `quality.no_face`, not dark). too_dark vs too_bright comes from the median luma. Two new provisional keys: `min_luma_ok` 0.8 and `max_saturated` 0.2.
- **G2: `assess(..., thresholds=)` takes the thresholds as a keyword** (the task's signature has no way to reach `quality:` otherwise); without it the file is loaded. `frame_w` is accepted but unused: motion is in face widths.
- **G2 → light.response: a step whose window is not usable is left out** (it counts as neither answered nor silent). Its reasons join the abstain when fewer than 4 steps remain, e.g. "6 had poor capture (the face was too dark to measure)". The fps and no-face abstains now come from the "all" window.
- **Test speed:** the server suite takes about 60 s now, because every light test renders its own synthetic session. Share the sessions with module-scoped fixtures at G11 if it gets in the way.
- **G3: "unclear vs silent" applies to light.response too, not only face_neck.** A step with no echo counts as silent only when its 4-sigma threshold is at most half the session's median answered echo, and at most `max_silent_threshold` (0.012, provisional). Otherwise it is unclear and left out. Without this, a synthetic genuine session at noise 0.06 was **flagged** (3 of 6 answered). Now it abstains with `light.too_few_steps` + `light.weak_face_echo`. **Trade-off:** a replay at synthetic noise 0.02 also abstains instead of flagging. `max_silent_threshold` is the number to set from real bright-room webcam sessions at S2 (REFERENCE: bright-room echo SNR ~5).
- **G3: `order_ok` is measured separately from the onset** (`echo.response`: mean chromaticity change over [painted + 200, ended + 100] ms, cosine with the shown colour ≥ 0.5) and only for steps that responded. A step that changes colour the wrong way is `light.wrong_order`, not `light.no_echo`. The synthetic `swap_colours=True` (each step echoes the colour half a sequence away) flags with `light.wrong_order`.
- **G3: `median_snr` is in threshold units** (hold amplitude / max(4 sigma, a_min)), so 1 is just detectable.
- **G3: fps below 12 and face present < 80% abstain before any step is measured** (`quality.low_fps`, `quality.no_face`). G2's window quality reasons are added once G2 exists.
- **G3: an echo outside the 0–300 ms band counts as a failed step** (the face did change, just not on time).
- **G4: dHash distance and frame difference are measured in `build_context`** (`hamming_prev`, `mad_prev`, against the previous *readable* frame), so frames are decoded once. A corrupt frame is never counted as a duplicate.
- **G4: `sensor.too_regular` flags (risk 0.7 vs flag_at 0.6), as the task says, but it is a hypothesis.** The old repo only measured evenness because hardware-timestamped webcams are very even too. At S2, check `exact_share` and `cv` on real webcams and phones before trusting it.
